The listing plan labels these "1st" to "4th floor". Every flight of stairs shares one stair core between the family room and the main-level hall.

## Assumptions to confirm

- **Lower-level windows under the addition.** The two windows on the recreation room's west wall now sit beneath the new suite and would look into its foundation or crawlspace.
- **Deck width.** The deck concept plan says 11'9", but the space between the dining-room wall and the garage's side-setback line is about 10'4". The model uses the narrower width so the deck stays inside the setback.
- **Route A stair** from the new deck to the existing lower deck drops about 13½ ft over about 14 ft. That is steep and needs a measured design.
- **Four-seasons ceiling.** It follows the near-flat roof from the street photo, about 8 ft high, instead of the vault drawn first.
- **Family-room-level bedroom window.** The listing plan shows a bay; the street photo shows a flat window. The model follows the photo.
- **Front door.** It shows wood on the outside (street photo) and white French doors on the inside (listing photo).

## Where things came from

- **Layout:** `assets/reference/floor-plans/original-listing-plan.png`, scaled from the room dimensions printed on it.
- **Addition and deck:** the two concept plans in the same folder.
- **Exterior style:** `assets/reference/photos/front-exterior-concept.jpg` (mansard upper floor, flat-roof brick addition, continuous hip roof over the main block and garage).
- **Interior finishes:** the listing photos.
- **Family room furniture:** `family-room-style-reference.jpg`.
- **Garage depth.** The listing plan gives the garage as 18'6" × 17'10", which would leave its doors about 2.8 ft behind the kitchen's front wall. The street photo shows the garage front flush with the kitchen, so the model brings the garage front out to the kitchen's line (about 20'7" deep inside) and carries the main roof's slope and white fascia straight across it. Width and the rear wall are unchanged.
