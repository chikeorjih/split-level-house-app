Worth trying

  • Current / Addition switches between today's house and the planned one.
  • Levels cut the house open; pick more than one to combine them.
  • Light goes from morning to evening; the Sun slider turns the sun around the house.
  • Quality: Fast for smooth movement, High by default, Ultra adds bounced light and reflections, Path traces the light for the most realistic stills.
  • Tools: jump to any room, rearrange the furniture, or turn on Debug IDs to point at any wall or piece.
